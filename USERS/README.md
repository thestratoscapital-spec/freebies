# 🚀 USERS: your launch week, 7 places to get your first users

You commented **USERS**. Here's the plan from the video: 7 places, in the order to use them, what to know about each, and a launch post you can copy and edit for each one.

**The rule for all 7:** be there to answer people. A launch is a conversation, not a link drop.

---

## The plan

| When | Place | Cost | The move that makes it work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before launch | [**BetaList**](https://betalist.com) | Paid plans | Collect early sign-ups before launch day |
| Day 1 | [**r/SideProject**](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/) | Free | Tell the story, ask what's broken. Read the subreddit rules first |
| Day 2 | [**Indie Hackers**](https://www.indiehackers.com) | Free | Share what you learned, then add it to the daily Build Board |
| Day 3 | [**Show HN**](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) (Hacker News) | Free | Title starts with "Show HN", let people try it without signing up, answer every comment |
| Day 4 | [**Product Hunt**](https://www.producthunt.com) | Free | Goes live at 12:01 AM Pacific and runs that full day. Smaller app? Weekends are quieter |
| Day 5 | [**Uneed**](https://www.uneed.best) | Free (or paid to pick your date) | Join the free line; enough upvotes earns a do-follow link back to your site |
| Bonus | [**DevHunt**](https://devhunt.org) | Free listing · $49 launch | Only for developer tools: weekly launches voted on by developers |

Facts checked on November 3, 2026 against each site's own pages (sources at the bottom). Sites change their rules and prices, so check again before you launch.

## Copy-and-edit launch posts

Replace everything in [brackets]. Keep it honest and in your own words.

### BetaList (pitch)
> **[App name]**: [what it does in one line, for who].
> [2–3 sentences: the problem, how your app fixes it, what makes it different.]

### r/SideProject
> **Title:** I built [app] because [the problem you had]. Would love honest feedback
>
> Hi all! I kept [problem], so I built [app]. It [what it does] in [simple steps].
> It's [free / has a free tier] here: [link]
> What's confusing or broken? I'm reading every comment and fixing things this week.

### Indie Hackers
> **Title:** What I learned building [app] in [time]
>
> [3 short lessons: what worked, what failed, one number you can share honestly.]
> If you want to try it: [link]. Happy to answer anything about how I built it.

### Show HN
> **Title:** Show HN: [App] – [what it does, plainly]
>
> I built [app] to [solve problem]. You can try it here without an account: [link]
> How it works: [1–2 technical sentences]. What I'd love feedback on: [specific question].
> I'll be here answering questions.

*Show HN rules: it must be something people can try (not a landing page or sign-up page), and don't ask friends to upvote.*

### Product Hunt (tagline + first comment)
> **Tagline:** [What it does in under 60 characters]
>
> **First comment:** Hey Product Hunt 👋 I'm [name], the maker of [app]. I built it because [problem].
> It helps you [3 benefits]. We're live all day and I'll answer every question. What should I build next?

### Uneed
> **[App name]**: [one-line pitch]. [One sentence on who it's for.] Try it: [link]

### DevHunt (developer tools only)
> **[Tool name]**: [what it does for developers]. Works with [stack]. Install: `[one command]`. Open source? [yes/no + repo link].

## Good to know

- **One per day.** Each place needs you around to reply. Don't launch everywhere at once.
- **Fix what Day 1 tells you** before Show HN and Product Hunt, where first impressions matter most.
- **Don't buy or swap upvotes.** Most of these sites ban it, and it can get your launch removed.
- The user numbers in the video are sample numbers to show the idea, not a result.
